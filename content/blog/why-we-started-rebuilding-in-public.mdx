Most business stories you hear are told in hindsight. The founder already knows how it ends, so the story gets shaped around that ending — every setback becomes a "lesson," every bad month becomes "part of the journey."

We wanted to try something different: talk about the rebuild *while it's happening*, before we know how it turns out.

## What this show actually is

Every episode, we sit down with someone who is mid-rebuild — a founder pivoting a business, an operator turning around a struggling team, someone starting over after something didn't work. We ask what's true *this week*, not what sounds good in retrospect.

That means:

- Numbers that aren't flattering yet
- Decisions that might be wrong
- Plans that will probably change by the next episode

## Why "in public"

Building quietly is safer. Nobody sees the version that doesn't work. But it also means nobody sees the actual process — the part where you don't know if the thing is going to work, and you do it anyway.

We think that part is the useful part.

If you're in the middle of rebuilding something — a company, a team, a product, a career — this show is for you. Not because we have answers, but because we're figuring it out at the same speed you are.
